DeepWork

Pomodoro & Focus — Privacy Policy

Short version

Your focus history stays on your phone. DeepWork has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not request the Internet permission.

Data the app stores

What the app creates while you use it: focus sessions and breaks (time, length, preset, tag, distractions, your rating and note), presets, tags, saved sound mixes, your own sounds, the creatures you have met in the Depths collection and app settings.

  • Stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • Deleted with “Delete all data” in Settings, or when you uninstall the app.

Your own sounds

When you add your own sound, you pick an audio file with the system file picker. The app copies it into its private storage, trims and normalizes it there. It gets access only to the file you pick — not to your other files, music or recordings.

Backups

  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include DeepWork's data in your device backup to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. This is controlled by Android and Google (Google Privacy Policy), not by us, and you can turn it off in system settings.
  • Backup file and CSV. “Back up to a file” and “Export sessions to CSV” create a file only where you choose to save it. “Restore from a file” reads only the file you pick. We never see these files.

Permissions

  • Notifications — the running timer and the end of each phase.
  • Alarms — so the end of a phase arrives on time even when the app is closed.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional, only if you turn it on) — to switch your phone to “alarms only” during focus and back afterwards. The app does not read your notifications.
  • Foreground service — only while a background sound is playing.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal information, accounts or sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or files (except a file you pick yourself).
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, crash-reporting SDKs or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising.

Google Play services

The app talks only to the Google Play Store app on your phone:

  • Google Play Billing — the one-time “DeepWork Pro” purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app never sees your payment details.
  • In-app updates and in-app review, provided by Google Play.

Widgets and quick settings tile

The home screen widgets show the timer and today's focus on your home screen, and the quick settings tile shows the time left — anyone who can see your screen can see them.

Sharing

“Share the week” creates an image on your device and shares it only when you tap it and choose where to send it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
